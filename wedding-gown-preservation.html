    Questions

    Will every stain come out?

    Not always. Some stains cannot be removed completely without damaging the fabric. We note the condition of your gown when you bring it in.

    Can beads and sequins be affected?

    Appliques, beads and decorative items may come off, and some lace, embroidery, beads and sequins can lose color or yellow over time, even with proper care.

    How soon should I bring my gown in?

    As soon as you can after the wedding. Fresh and invisible stains are easier to treat.

    Does the age of my dress matter?

    Yes. Gowns more than 20 years old need restoration, which is an additional charge. Choose the Restoration kit.

    How much is my gown covered for?

    Your gown is covered for $2,000 with the Clean Only, Traditional, Restoration and Flower Girl / Christening kits, and $3,000 with the Celebrity kit. You can add $1,000, $2,000 or $3,000 more coverage when you order. Read the full release.

    How do you care for my gown?

    We clean your gown following the manufacturer's care label and note its condition when we receive it. Delicate fabrics, beads, sequins and older stains can react to cleaning, so we ask you to review and sign a short release before we start. Read the full release.

    What if my gown has no care label?

    We are not responsible for gowns or accessories without a manufacturer's care label and fiber content. Once you have signed the release, our expertise will use the process best suited for your gown. Read the full release.

    Do accessories change the guarantee?

    Sashes, belts, beads or sequins added or altered by anyone other than the original manufacturer void any guarantee. Read the full release.
