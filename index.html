Welcome

Custom Cleaners is a family owned business, established in January 1996 with a simple mission: to provide every customer with quality service.

Over the years, we have grown into one of the most trusted cleaners in Baytown, Texas. Our success is reflected in the kind words and referrals we receive from our valued, loyal customers.

We invite you to experience our friendly service and quality cleaning firsthand, and to make us your cleaner. Our goal is to put a smile on your face and give you confidence in how you look, because we truly value our customers.

Dry Cleaning & Laundry Services in Baytown

Same Day Service

Same day dry cleaning and laundry service is available upon request.

Garth Rd: Available Monday through Friday. Items received by 8:00am are typically ready by 5:00pm the same day. Certain restrictions may apply.

Hwy 146: Same day service is not available at this location.

Dry Cleaning

Every garment is dry cleaned with exceptional care. We use EcoSolv® dry cleaning fluid, an environmentally safe solution that is gentle on your clothing and on the environment.

Shirt Laundering

Our laundry service keeps your whites bright and your colors vibrant. We pre-treat every collar and cuff, remove all removable stains, and replace commonly broken buttons free of charge.

Starching for shirts and jeans is included at no additional cost, with light, medium, and heavy options available.

Alterations

Our expert seamstress handles all of your alteration needs. Most alterations are completed within 5 business days, and expedited service is available upon request.

Leather and Suede

Like any garment, leather requires regular care and cleaning. Our expert leather cleaning brings aged, lusterless leather back to a fresh look and feel, and we restore dull leather and suede to like-new condition.

Most leather and suede items are cleaned within 7 business days. Get ready for cold weather now.

Uniform and Apparel Cleaning

We provide cleaning services to school districts in the surrounding areas. Pickup and delivery is available to school districts.

Special Fabrics

All special and delicate garments require tender care. An experienced dry cleaner can clean your garments with care and expertise. We handle a wide variety of special garments, so come in and ask us how we can help.

Wedding Gown Heirloom

Preserve the memories of your wedding day. Your gown is professionally cleaned and sealed in an airtight preservation box that protects it from mold, dust, and fading for years to come. It may even become a cherished heirloom to pass down to the next generation.

Drapes and Curtains

Springtime cleaning or holiday season cleaning, we are here to clean your drapes and curtains so your home can look its best.

Comforters

Whether it is time for your kids to go away to college or you are getting ready for chilly weather, cleaning your comforter on a regular basis helps keep you and your family away from nasty germs. We can launder or dry clean your comforter based on your preference or its care label.

Linens

Expecting guests? Bring us your linens, tablecloths, and napkins. We clean and press them for you, so you can spend your time on what you care about most: family and friends.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for customers in Baytown, Mont Belvieu, Highlands, and Crosby.

Where is Custom Cleaners located in Baytown, TX?

We have two Baytown locations: 4517 Garth Rd, Baytown, TX 77521, and 7123 N Highway 146, Baytown, TX 77521. Directions and phone numbers are in our Locations section.

What are your hours?

Both locations are open Monday through Friday from 6:30am to 7:00pm and Saturday from 8:00am to 5:00pm. We are closed on Sunday.

Do you serve Mont Belvieu, Highlands, and Crosby?

Our two Baytown locations are convenient to Mont Belvieu, Highlands, and Crosby, and we welcome customers from all of these communities.

Do you offer same day dry cleaning in Baytown?

Same day dry cleaning and laundry service is available upon request at our Garth Rd location, Monday through Friday. Items received by 8:00am are typically ready by 5:00pm, and certain restrictions may apply. Same day service is not available at our Highway 146 location.

How long do alterations take?

Our expert seamstress handles all of your alteration needs. Most alterations are completed within 5 business days, and expedited service is available upon request.

Do you clean and preserve wedding gowns?

Yes. We are an authorized Wedding Gown Preservation Co. dealer. Your gown is cleaned and sealed in an airtight preservation box that helps protect it from mold, dust, and fading.

Is starch included when you launder shirts?

Yes. Starching for shirts and jeans is included at no additional cost, with light, medium, and heavy options. We also pre-treat every collar and cuff and replace commonly broken buttons free of charge.

Do you clean leather and suede?

Yes. We clean leather and suede, and most items are cleaned within 7 business days.

What do you use to dry clean clothes?

We use EcoSolv dry cleaning fluid, an environmentally safe solution that is gentle on your clothing and on the environment.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Pickup and delivery is available to school districts. Please call either location to ask about pickup and delivery for your needs.

How do I join your rewards program or log in to my account?

Join our rewards program from the Specials section to receive monthly and seasonal discounts and specials by text and email. Existing customers can use the Customer Login button to sign in to their account.
